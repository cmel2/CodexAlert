Your data

Privacy, plainly.

What we process

When you add Discord, we receive the webhook URL you submit, an encrypted stored version, a keyed duplicate fingerprint, a hashed unsubscribe token, and delivery health data. A keyed request fingerprint helps limit abuse. We do not store raw IP addresses.

Why we use it

That data lets us validate the route, send the test and reset alerts you request, avoid duplicate subscriptions, and protect the public endpoints. Webhook credentials are encrypted before storage and never returned to the browser.

Providers and sharing

Supabase provides the database and server infrastructure. Discord receives the messages needed to deliver your alert. We do not sell personal data or require a name, email address, Discord login, OAuth permission, or message history.

Your control

Your private unsubscribe link deactivates the route and erases its stored webhook credential. Keep that link private: anyone with it can remove the subscription. Reset data comes from hascodexratelimitreset.today, created by @jskoiz; it may be delayed or inaccurate.
