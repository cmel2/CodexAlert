Privacy, plainly.

CodexAlert is designed to deliver one useful signal without asking for an account.

What we store

When you subscribe, we store the Discord webhook URL in encrypted form, a keyed duplicate fingerprint, a hashed unsubscribe token, and the delivery state needed to keep alerts reliable.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, Discord login, OAuth permission, or message history.

How it is used

The webhook is used to send the confirmation and future reset notifications you requested. It is never returned to the browser. Unsubscribing erases the stored credential.

Infrastructure and sharing

Supabase hosts the database and server functions. Discord receives a message only when a delivery is due. We do not sell personal data.

Source limitation

Reset data comes from hascodexratelimitreset.today, created by @jskoiz. It is third-party information and may be delayed or inaccurate.
