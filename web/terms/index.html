Before you start

Terms of use.

Use a route you control

Only submit a Discord incoming webhook you own or are authorized to manage. Keep the private unsubscribe link private; anyone with it can deactivate that route.

What the service does

CodexAlert watches a community-maintained reset signal and sends a Discord message when it appears new. It is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or Discord.

Limits of the signal

The source is third-party and may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. Alerts are best effort only: we do not promise delivery, timing, availability, or that any Codex limit has actually reset.

Reasonable use

Do not submit someone else’s route, use the service to send unwanted content, probe other systems, or evade platform limits. We may suspend or remove a route to protect the service or comply with law.

External services and changes

Your use of Discord and the reset source remains subject to their own terms. We may change, pause, or discontinue CodexAlert. It is provided “as is” to the extent permitted by law, without limiting rights that cannot be limited.
