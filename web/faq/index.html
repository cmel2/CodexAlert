General

CodexAlert watches the community reset tracker and sends a Discord notification when a new reset is reported.

Was this answer helpful?

No. It is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or Discord.

One shared check runs every minute for all subscribers. It does not poll once per subscriber.

The source is third-party and may be delayed or inaccurate. Notifications say limits “appear” to have reset.

Channels

Choose Discord, paste an incoming webhook you control, and select Add Discord Webhook. A test message is sent before the route is saved.

Telegram is a planned route and is marked coming soon until its delivery backend is implemented.

Slack is a planned route and is marked coming soon until its delivery backend is implemented.

Privacy

An encrypted webhook URL, a keyed duplicate fingerprint, a hashed unsubscribe token, and delivery health state. No name, email, Discord login, or OAuth.

Use the private unsubscribe link shown after setup. Anyone with the link can remove the subscription, so keep it private.
